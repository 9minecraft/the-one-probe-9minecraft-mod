# The One Probe — Never Guess Mob Health or Required Tools

The One Probe 9minecraft Mod puts a small information box on your screen when you look at things in the game. You just point your crosshair at any block, machine, or mob to see exactly what it is. This mod stops you from guessing which tool you need to break a block or how much health a monster has left. It reads data from the world and shows you the block name, the mod it belongs to, and the right tool to use. You can also see inside chests or check how much power your machines have without opening their menus.

## What this build is

An unofficial rebuild of **McJtyMods**'s original work, kept running on current Minecraft releases by 9Minecraft Studios. Not affiliated with the original author and not endorsed by them.

Most of the porting work sits in the mixin layer. Each Minecraft release moves or renames the methods this mod hooks into, so the hooks are re-pointed and re-tested against that version rather than rewritten. The behaviour underneath is upstream's and is deliberately left alone — that is the part that makes the mod feel right, and it is not ours to redesign.
